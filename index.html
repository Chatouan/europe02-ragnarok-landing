Before you play

What to know about your account, PC and the game.

Is it free to play?

Yes. Ragnarok: The New World is free to play and includes in-app purchases.

Can I play on my PC?

This page is for the Windows PC journey through Steam. The Steam page lists 12 GB RAM, 30 GB available space and a broadband connection as minimum requirements. Check the current requirements on Steam before you begin.

Do I need a Gravity account?

Yes. The Steam page states that you need to create a Gravity account through the game launcher. Your Steam account and Gravity account are separate.

Does the game include auto-battle?

Yes. The official game introduction describes auto-battle and offline resource accumulation. This version includes automated progression features.

What happens after I click?

The offer opens in a new tab. Continue from there to prepare the game and sign in with the required account. Clicking does not start a playable session.

Can I change my class?

The official Steam description says one character can change classes and reset skills. Explore the available classes to find the approach you prefer.
